🥡 Tenpax Lunch

A lunch-ordering app for 10 coworkers: start a lunch run, everyone adds their order, and the organizer tracks the total and who has paid.

What the demo shows

  1. A coworker joins with the shared team code.
  2. They start today's lunch run and add their order.
  3. Everyone else's orders appear in one list with the total.
  4. The organizer ticks off who has paid and closes orders at the cutoff.
